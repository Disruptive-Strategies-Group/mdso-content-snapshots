The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Training Center (MDSTC) serves as a Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) Criminal Justice Standards and Training Commission regional training center. MDSTC is a certifying agency for both Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office (MDSO) and Miami-Dade Corrections and Rehabilitation (MDCR) academies.
