How to Report Public Corruption

The Public Corruption Section investigates allegations of corruption involving public officials and employees within Miami-Dade County. If you have information regarding public corruption, you can report it by:

  • Calling the Public Corruption Tip Line at 305-471-3400
  • Emailing publiccorruption@mdso.com
  • Calling Miami-Dade Crime Stoppers at 305-371-TIPS (7867)

What Is Public Corruption?

Public corruption involves the misuse of public office or position for personal gain. Examples include bribery, kickback schemes, bid rigging, fraud, embezzlement, extortion, and conflicts of interest involving public officials or employees.

All reports are taken seriously and investigated thoroughly. You may remain anonymous.
