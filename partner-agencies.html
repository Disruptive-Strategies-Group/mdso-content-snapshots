Miami-Dade Corrections and Rehabilitation Department

MDSTC is pleased to welcome the Miami-Dade Corrections and Rehabilitation (MDCR) Department as a partner in this shared-training initiative. Together, we are committed to fostering a collaborative environment that enhances public safety and strengthens our community.

Our Mission
We, the Miami-Dade County Corrections and Rehabilitation Department, serve our community by providing safe, secure, and humane detention of individuals in our custody while preparing them for a successful return to the community.

Our Vision
We envision a unified workforce of progressive, dedicated professionals grounded in service, integrity, and pride. Our team strives to provide caring and compassionate services to those entrusted to our care and to protect the public we serve.
