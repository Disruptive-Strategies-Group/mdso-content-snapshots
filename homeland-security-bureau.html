The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office Homeland Security Bureau (HSB) is comprised of five sections: Intelligence and Investigations, Priority Response Team, Southeast Florida Fusion Center, Real Time Crime Center, and the Southeast Regional Domestic Security Task Force. Each section has a specific task and combined they augment and enhance the Department's capability to respond and address the growing homeland security initiatives.

While the HSB's primary jurisdictional responsibility is unincorporated areas of Miami-Dade County, personnel provide homeland security and criminal intelligence gathering and dissemination assistance to law enforcement agencies at the local, regional, state, federal, and international levels.

Intelligence & Investigation Section (IIS)

The IIS are the primary departmental detectives on investigations encompassing threats of mass shootings, threats to infrastructure, or threats to public safety.

Southeast Florida Fusion Center (SEFFC)

The SEFFC focuses on the development and exchange of criminal intelligence, and provides a timely exchange of information regarding crime or criminal related activity within the law enforcement community. The SEFFC's purpose is to enhance partnerships which establish connections between each facet of the law enforcement community.

Real-Time Crime Center (RTCC)

The RTCC functions as a centralized data hub that rapidly mines information across multiple criminal, civil, and social media sources. Such information includes video and still imagery, data analysis, and access to ShotSpotter, a gunshot detection, location, alert, and analysis solution deployed across Miami-Dade County.
