The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Training Center is home to four advanced firearm ranges offering ample space on either side of the target pad to utilize a variety of portable target and obstacle structures.
Range one is a 50-yard basic qualification range. It features:
  • 24 turning target positions
  • Fully automated range tower controls
  • On-range remote controls
Range two is a 50-yard qualification range. It features:
  • 24 turning target positions
  • Fully automated range tower controls
  • On-range remote controls
  • A running man that spans all 24 positions.
Range three is MDSTC's 100-yard tactical range with:
  • 30 turning target positions
  • Running man
  • Pop-up steel targets
Range four is MDSTC's 200-yard tactical rifle range. It features:
  • Covered cement staging
  • Configurable target area
