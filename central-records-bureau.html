The Central Records Bureau maintains and distributes all public records for Miami-Dade County, including arrest forms, police reports and Florida Traffic Crash Reports.

Police Clearance Letters

The Bureau also provides Police Clearance Letters, detailing criminal history information, for the purpose of:

  • Citizenship and immigration
  • Employment
  • Adoption
  • Education/school
  • Investigation
  • Legal/lawyer/court
  • Personal reasons
