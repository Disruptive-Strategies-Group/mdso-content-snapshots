Mission Statement

Our Mission

The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office protects and serves Miami-Dade County with honor, integrity, and professionalism — partnering with the community to ensure law and order, safety, and trust through fair and transparent efforts.

Vision Statement

Our Vision

Our vision is to set the highest standard as a Sheriff's Office, which the community proudly supports with mutually entrusted goals.

MDSO Core Values

Our Core Values

I

Integrity

Upholding the highest ethical standards with honesty in all actions to foster trust and credibility with the community.

R

Respect

Treating all persons in a dignified and courteous manner, both in our professional and personal endeavors.

A

Accountability

Taking responsibility for actions and decisions, maintaining transparency and serving the needs of the community.

S

Service

Protecting our community by providing law and order with professionalism and compassion.
