The Community Education Unit services over 100 Miami-Dade County Schools, from Pre-K to 8th grade. The CEU offers safety presentations to students and seniors in our community, reaching over 100,000 citizens each year.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide traffic and safety knowledge to children and adults, with the sole purpose of saving lives and preventing injuries. We take pride in providing a courteous and efficient service to the citizens of Miami-Dade County. We uphold the principles and policies of Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office.
