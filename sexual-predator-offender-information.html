Registration Requirements

Sexual predators and offenders are required to register with the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office and comply with all applicable Florida statutes regarding residency restrictions, reporting obligations, and registration updates.

For registration inquiries, contact the Sexual Predator/Offender Registration Unit at 305-471-2800.
