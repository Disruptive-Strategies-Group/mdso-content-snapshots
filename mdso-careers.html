A career with the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office offers an opportunity for human involvement, professional advancement and community service. MDSO is charged with the safety of over 2.5 million residents who live within a 2,109-square-mile area. In order to accomplish this formidable task, the Department utilizes the services of approximately 2,800 sworn officers and 1,500 support personnel.

The Sheriff's Office is considered among the most progressive in the country, an organization that welcomes change and is eager to meet new challenges.

Tuition Reimbursement

Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office policy provides that employees enrolled in accredited educational institutions may be reimbursed for 50 percent of tuition costs, for approved coursework which will enable them to improve their performance in their current positions and prepare them for increased responsibilities. The Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office encourages our employees to take advantage of this upon completing their probation.
