Press Release

June 4, 2026 — Media Contact: Det. Samantha Choon, samantha.choon@mdso.us / 305-471-1900

On Tuesday, June 2, 2026, at approximately 7:32 p.m., Doral Police Officers responded to the area of NW 111 Court and NW 72 Terrace regarding a call to check on the welfare.

Upon arrival, officers entered the residence and located an unresponsive adult female, unresponsive adult male, and two unresponsive juvenile females.

Miami-Dade Fire Rescue responded and pronounced the four individuals deceased on scene.

Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office (MDSO) Homicide Bureau detectives responded to the scene and assumed the investigation. No further information is available at this time.

Update #1

The Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office Homicide Bureau investigation is currently leading detectives to believe this incident was a murder-suicide.
