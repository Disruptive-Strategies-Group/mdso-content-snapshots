This notice applies to all official Miami-Dade County websites, and describes what information we collect, how we use that information and your options regarding information collections.

Information We Collect

When you visit County web sites, we may collect some information from you. This information does not identify you personally and may include: your Internet protocol address, the type of browser and operating system used to access the site, the date and time the site was accessed, the pages visited, and if you linked from another web site, the address of that site.

If you voluntarily provide information to us through an e-mail or online form, we may use that information to respond to you, to send updates, or to conduct the County business.

Use of Cookies

Some County applications use "cookies." A cookie is a small data file transferred to your hard drive that allows a web site to respond to you as an individual. The cookie records information about your online preferences and allows us to tailor our services to your interests. You can set your browser to disable cookies. If you do so, however, some parts of County web sites may not function properly.

Security

Miami-Dade County uses industry-standard software to protect the confidentiality of personally identifiable information. However, absolute security of online transactions cannot be guaranteed due to the inherent nature of the Internet.

Public Records

Under Florida law, e-mail addresses are public records. If you do not want your e-mail address released in response to a public records request, do not send electronic mail to this entity. Instead, contact the office by phone or in writing.

Links to Other Sites

County web sites contain links to other web sites. The County is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of other sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of sites you visit.

If you have questions about this privacy notice, please contact Miami-Dade County at 311 or 305-375-4636.
