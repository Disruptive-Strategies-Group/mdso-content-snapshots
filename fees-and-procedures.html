Non-Enforceable Writs

Summons, 5-Day Summons, Eminent Domain, Injunctions, Notices, Writs of Garnishment, Rules to Show Cause, Notices of Tax Sale, Subpoenas, and all other types of non-enforceable process requiring service only.

Service Fee: $40 for each service per address plus a self-addressed stamped envelope.

Requirements: Original or certified copy, one conformed copy and attachments/complaints for each party or entity to be served

Out of State

This office will serve the process unless an explicit letter of instructions accompanies each document.

Service Fee: $41 for each service per address and additional in-state process charges that are not required by Florida, but are required by the originating state

Enforceable Civil Writs

All documents must be either issued in or domesticated (adopted and certified) within the State of Florida.

The Sheriff's Office reserves the right to request that the plaintiff or their representative obtain additional court orders or clarification from the court prior to any process or serving of writs.

Any enforceable process which takes more than one hour to execute will be charged at the rate of $70.80 per hour per Deputy Sheriff. If anticipated, an estimated cost deposit will be required in addition to the original cost deposit. Expedited service is available and the plaintiff or agent requesting such service can call us for a quote for prices that may exceed what is delineated on this page.

  • Writ of Possession
    Total Fee: $115 per writ per address, plus a self-addressed stamped envelope.
  • Landlord / Tenant Requirements:
    Original writ, one conformed copy
  • Foreclosure Requirements:
    Original writ, one conformed copy of writ

All Writs of Possession must have name and telephone number of landlord/authorized agent who will meet the Deputy Sheriff and accept possession of the premises.

  • Writ of Replevin
    Requirements: Original writ and one conformed copy. The writ must contain the address of the property under replevy and the name of a contact person to turn the property over to in the field. The plaintiff must provide labor and equipment necessary to complete the replevin.
  • Fee: $90 per address plus self-addressed stamped envelope
  • If you wish to have a summons served at the same time the Writ of Replevin is served, you must send an additional fee for the service of the summons:
  • Fee: $40 per summons per address
  • Distress Writ
    Requirements: Certified copy of writ and a conformed copy, levy instructions, description of the property under distress and its location
  • Fee: $90 per address plus self-addressed stamped envelope (cost deposit may be required)
  • Writ of Execution
    Total Fee: $710
  • $40.00 per service per address
  • $50.00 levy
  • $40.00 each advertisement of sale under process
  • $40.00 each sale under process
  • $40.00 each Bill of Sale, Satisfaction of judgment
  • $500.00 minimum cost deposit

Instructions for Levy

An Instructions for Levy form must be accompanied by a cost deposit. Instructions for Levy forms are available at our office.

  • 1: Cost deposits are collected in advance
    Vehicle - $500
    The Instructions for Levy form must be accompanied by a DMV printout, which can be obtained by writing to:
    Department of Motor Vehicles
    Neil Kirkman Building
    Tallahassee, FL 32301
    The name(s) on the DMV printout must match those on the Writ of Execution EXACTLY. If they do not, you must furnish an Affidavit of Same Name, stating the reason you believe that they are one and the same.
  • 2: Real Estate - $500
    Recording Fee (payable to the Miami-Dade Clerk of Courts) - $10
    The Instructions for Levy form must contain the legal description of the property. If the location is a vacant lot, you must furnish complete and specific directions to the property. A certified copy of the Final Judgment must be recorded with the Clerk of the Circuit Court in order to establish priority. A certified copy of the Warranty Deed and a certified recorded copy of the Final Judgment must be provided.
  • 3: Airplane/boat/household items/business inventory, etc.
    Cost deposits on these types of items are determined on an individual basis. Call us to obtain the amount and have available all the specifics involved with your levy: type of property, location, how much property is to be seized, etc.
    These cost deposits are an estimate of the costs (sheriff's fees, towing and storage fees, advertising fees, recording fees, etc.) involved with a levy.
    Should the actual costs incurred be greater than the cost deposit, the plaintiff and/or attorney directing the levy will be responsible for any additional charges.
