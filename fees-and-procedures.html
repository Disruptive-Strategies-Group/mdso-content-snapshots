Non-Enforceable Writs

Summons, 5-Day Summons, Eminent Domain, Injunctions, Notices, Writs of Garnishment, Rules to Show Cause, Notices of Tax Sale, Subpoenas, and all other types of non-enforceable process requiring service only.

Service Fee: $40 for each service per address plus a self-addressed stamped envelope.

Requirements: Original or certified copy, one conformed copy and attachments/complaints for each party or entity to be served

Enforceable Civil Writs

All documents must be either issued in or domesticated (adopted and certified) within the State of Florida.

The Sheriff's Office reserves the right to request that the plaintiff or their representative obtain additional court orders or clarification from the court prior to any process or serving of writs.

Any enforceable process which takes more than one hour to execute will be charged at the rate of $70.80 per hour per Deputy Sheriff. If anticipated, an estimated cost deposit will be required in addition to the original cost deposit. Expedited service is available and the plaintiff or agent requesting such service can call us for a quote for prices that may exceed what is delineated on this page.

  • Writ of Possession
    Total Fee: $115 per writ per address, plus a self-addressed stamped envelope.
  • Landlord / Tenant Requirements:
    Original writ, one conformed copy
  • Foreclosure Requirements:
    Original writ, one conformed copy of writ

All Writs of Possession must have name and telephone number of landlord/authorized agent who will meet the Deputy Sheriff and accept possession of the premises.

Civil Warrants, Orders of Commitment, Capias, Writs of Bodily Attachment, etc.

All Warrants issued from a County outside of Miami-Dade MUST be forwarded to our office by the Sheriff's office of the issuing County.
