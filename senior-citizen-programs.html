The safety of our elderly community is important to us due to the rise in elderly pedestrian fatalities and falling victim to identity theft. The CEU conducts safety presentations at Senior Citizen Centers and Senior Adult Organizations on topics below:
  • Identity Theft
  • Pedestrian Safety for Elderly
