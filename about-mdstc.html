The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Training Center (MDSTC) is located on a 35-acre campus in Doral, Florida and serves as a Florida Department of Law Enforcement (FDLE) Criminal Justice Standards and Training Commission regional training center. MDSTC is a certifying agency for both Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office (MDSO) and Miami-Dade Corrections and Rehabilitation (MDCR) academies. Training is provided for local, state and federal law enforcement, and criminal justice agencies within the United States and from around the world.

  • Corrections and Public Service Aide Academies
  • In-Service Training
  • Civilian Training
  • Specialized and Technical Training
  • Management Training
  • Executive Level Training
  • Three Advanced Firearm Ranges
  • Tactical Shoot-House
  • Challenging Physical Agility Course
  • Computer Lab
  • Fully Equipped Classrooms with Smartboards
  • Gymnasium and Regulation Basketball Court
  • Aquatic Training Center
  • Fitness Center
  • Outdoor Training Spaces
  • Conference Room
  • Interactive Shooting Simulator

Information for Vendors

The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Training Center is an operating entity of the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office and of Miami-Dade County, Florida. The training center is not the purchasing entity, although we often participate in product demonstrations and testing activities.  

Vendors who wish to demonstrate their products to the MDSTC must first contact the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Finance & Procurement Office at 305-471-2520 to request a product demonstration at our facility. Any vendor who wishes to conduct business with the MDSTC must register with the Miami-Dade County Strategic Procurement Department.
