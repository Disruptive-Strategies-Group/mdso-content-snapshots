Residence Address Compliance

The Lauren Book Child Safety Ordinance made it against the law for certain sexual offenders and sexual predators to live within 2,500 feet of any school in Miami-Dade County. To read more about this and other restrictions in Miami-Dade County please review the Lauren Book Child Safety Ordinance.

Miami-Dade County provides a 2,500-Foot Address Compliance Map that displays sexual predators in the area, as well as schools, parks and day care centers.

The information shown in the map is received from multiple sources. Miami-Dade County does not assume responsibility for the accuracy or timeliness of the information displayed. The map should only be used as a guide.
