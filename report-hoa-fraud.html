The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office is dedicated to investigating complaints of fraud and financial misconduct within homeowners' associations (HOAs) and condominium associations. If you suspect fraudulent activity in your community, we encourage you to file a report.

Report HOA or Condo Fraud

Use the form below to submit your report. All information provided will be reviewed by the appropriate investigative unit.

Submit HOA Fraud Report

What to Include in Your Report

To help investigators review your complaint, please include as much detail as possible:

  • Name and address of the HOA or condominium association
  • Names of individuals involved
  • Description of the suspected fraudulent activity
  • Any supporting documentation (financial records, meeting minutes, contracts, etc.)
  • Your contact information for follow-up
