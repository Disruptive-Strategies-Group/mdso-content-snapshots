Miami-Dade Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz has tasked the Organized Crime Bureau to increase investigations of Homeowner’s Association and Condo fraud throughout Miami-Dade County.

HOA and Condo Fraud has become prevalent in our County and that is why Sheriff Rosie has made combating this type of fraud a priority. If you suspect fraud within your HOA or Condo Association, we want to hear from you.

How to Report

If you believe you have information about HOA or Condo fraud, you can report it by:

  • Calling the Organized Crime Bureau at 305-471-2500
  • Emailing hoafraud@mdso.com
  • Calling Miami-Dade Crime Stoppers at 305-371-TIPS (7867)

What Constitutes HOA/Condo Fraud

  • Misappropriation of association funds
  • Fraudulent financial reporting
  • Kickback schemes with vendors
  • Election rigging or manipulation
  • Self-dealing by board members
