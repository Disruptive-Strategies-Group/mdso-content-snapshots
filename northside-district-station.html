The Northside District works in close partnership with the community to reduce crime, enhance prevention efforts, and increase public awareness, all with the goal of improving quality of life. The District employs intelligence-led policing strategies and directed patrols to proactively address crime trends.

Specialized units focus on identifying, solving, and reducing specific criminal activity, including group violence, burglaries, auto theft, illegal pawn operations, and repeat offenders. The District continually evaluates and refines its enforcement strategies and community protection initiatives to adapt to evolving community needs and emerging crime patterns.
