Press Release

May 12, 2026 — Media Contact: Det. Joseph R. Peguero Rivera, joseph.peguerorivera@mdso.us / 305-471-1900

On Tuesday, May 12, 2026, at approximately 3:53 p.m., deputies from the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office (MDSO) South District responded to the area of 10143 Circle Plaza West in reference to a child who had been shot.

According to investigators, the mother heard a loud popping sound from inside the residence and found her son suffering from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound to his upper extremity. The mother transported the child to Jackson South Medical Center, where he was later airlifted by Miami-Dade Fire Rescue to HCA Florida Kendall Hospital. He is currently listed in stable condition.

MDSO South District General Investigations Unit detectives responded and assumed the investigation.

No further information at this time.
