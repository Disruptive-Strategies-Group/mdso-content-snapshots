Press Release

February 26, 2026 — Media Contact: Det. Samantha Choon, samantha.choon@mdso.us / 305-471-1900

The Florida Department of Law Enforcement is investigating a shooting involving a deputy from the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office (MDSO).

On Tuesday, February 24, 2026, just after 8:00 p.m., MDSO deputies from the Robbery Intervention Team (RID) attempted to initiate a traffic stop in the area of NW 17 Ave and NW 71 Street. The vehicle fled, however MDSO aviation units were overhead.

MDSO aviation units observed the subject vehicle strike a civilian vehicle in the area of NW 17 Ave and NW 87 Street. The subject vehicle came to a stop in the roadway. Deputies arrived and approached the vehicle, at which time the subject exited the vehicle armed.

A deputy discharged his service weapon, striking the subject in the lower extremities.
