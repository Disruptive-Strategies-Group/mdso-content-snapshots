The Midwest District emphasizes service to the community through proactive strategies aimed at combating crime and enhancing public safety. Through focused enforcement activities and a zero-tolerance approach toward criminal behavior, the District targets crimes that have the most impact on residents and businesses.

District personnel work diligently to reduce residential and commercial burglaries through the use of new technologies and coordinated efforts with the Residential Burglary, Commercial Burglary, and Property Crimes Squads. These combined efforts strengthen crime-prevention initiatives and improve response effectiveness.
