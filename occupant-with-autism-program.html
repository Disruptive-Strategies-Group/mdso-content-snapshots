The Occupant with Autism Program is a free decal program designed to alert law enforcement officers and first responders that a vehicle occupant may have autism or a related developmental disability.

About the Program

The decal is placed on the rear window of a vehicle to notify officers during a traffic stop or other encounter that a person inside the car may have difficulty communicating or may exhibit unexpected behaviors. This awareness helps officers adjust their approach and ensure a safer interaction for everyone involved.

How to Participate

Decals are available at no cost. To obtain a decal, visit any Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office district station or contact the Community Affairs Bureau.
