The Lauren Book Child Safety Ordinance made it against the law for a sexual predator to live within 2,500 feet of any school. Miami-Dade County provides a 2,500-Foot Radius Address Compliance Map that displays sexual predators in the area, as well as schools, parks and day care centers.
